Blob & Disk Storage, explained from scratch

Store unstructured data and VM disks. Learn access tiers, redundancy and lifecycle rules.

What is Blob Storage?

Blob Storage is for unstructured data: documents, images, videos, logs, backups. Unlike a database, it doesn't care about structure — just store bytes. You organize blobs into containers (like folders). Access via REST API, Azure SDK, or direct HTTP. Extremely scalable: petabytes of data for pennies per month.

Access Tiers

  • Hot — for frequently accessed data (websites, APIs). Higher storage cost, lower access cost.
  • Cool — accessed infrequently (archives, long-term backups). Lower storage cost, higher retrieval cost.
  • Archive — rarely accessed (compliance archives, long-term retention). Cheapest storage, slowest retrieval (hours), can't access until "rehydrated".
Move data between tiers as usage changes — a common pattern: store hot, then move to cool, then archive.

Redundancy & Replication

Choose redundancy for durability:

  • LRS (Local Redundant Storage) — 3 copies in one datacenter. Cheapest, fastest; survives disk failure.
  • GRS (Geo-Redundant Storage) — copies in two regions. Survives entire region failure.
  • ZRS (Zone-Redundant Storage) — copies across zones in one region. Survives zone failure.
Higher redundancy = more cost. Choose based on RTO/RPO needs.

Lifecycle Management

Automatically move blobs between tiers or delete them based on rules. Example: after 30 days, move from hot to cool; after 90 days, move to archive; after 7 years, delete. Lifecycle policies run daily at UTC midnight. Perfect for cost optimization: keep hot data hot for speed, age it out automatically.

Getting Started

Create a Storage Account: Portal → Storage accounts → Create. Choose redundancy (LRS for dev, GRS for production). Create a container. Upload blobs via Portal, CLI, or SDK. Set access tier. Add a lifecycle policy: Portal → Storage account → Lifecycle management → Add rule. Monitor costs in Cost Management. Use SAS tokens or Managed Identity for apps to access blobs securely.
